NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Field Shift

Make every square meter count.

FieldShift helps smallholder farmers make every square meter count by turning NASA Earth observations, local soil and market data, and field sensors into explainable crop choices and automated resource actions.

What we're optimizing

Not expanding land, and not rebuilding the whole supply chain. FieldShift optimizes the decisions a farmer can still control: what to plant, when to plant it, how much water and input to use, and when to act.

Crop selection Crop rotation Net margin / ha Water efficiency Explainable recommendation IoT action loop
North-star metric
Risk-adjusted net margin per hectare

Not simply chasing the highest yield. The system looks for the best net income within acceptable water, climate, soil, and market risk limits.

How it works

From data → decision → action → learning.

The platform combines Earth observation, field profile, local economics, and field sensors. A scenario engine computes the options; an LLM explains the results in plain language; IoT helps carry out the action.

1. Farmer & FieldField polygon, previous crops, irrigation, capital, risk preference.
2. NASA & Local DataVegetation, rainfall, historical climate, soil, calendar, cost, and local prices.
3. IoT Ground TruthSoil moisture, microclimate, water flow, pump status.
4. Scenario EngineTest agronomic feasibility and rotation for each crop.
5. Margin & RiskCompute net income, water needs, and risk penalties.
6. LLM ExplainabilityExplains reasons, trade-offs, and next steps; not the primary scoring driver.
7. Action LoopAlerts, irrigation control, outcome monitoring, and recommendation updates.
