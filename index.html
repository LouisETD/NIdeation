NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Field Shift

Make every square meter count.

FieldShift helps smallholder farmers make every square meter count by turning NASA Earth observations, local soil and market data, and field sensors into explainable crop choices and a one-season stress test. Read-only field sensors provide ground truth; farmers review every plan.

What we're optimizing

Not expanding land, and not rebuilding the whole supply chain. FieldShift optimizes the decisions a farmer can still choose: what to plant next, how much water and capital a plan needs, and how margin changes under stated assumptions.

Crop selection Crop rotation Net margin / ha Water efficiency Explainable recommendation IoT ground truth Stress Lab
North-star metric
Risk-adjusted net margin per hectare

Not simply chasing the highest yield. The system looks for the best net income within acceptable water, climate, soil, and market risk limits.

How it works

From field evidence → feasible plan → farmer review.

NASA and local data frame the season; a transparent engine checks soil, calendar, water, and capital, then ranks feasible crops by margin after an explicit downside reserve. IoT records ground truth only.

1. Farmer & FieldField polygon, previous crops, irrigation, capital, risk preference.
2. NASA & Local DataVegetation, rainfall, historical climate, soil, calendar, cost, and local prices.
3. IoT Ground TruthSoil moisture, microclimate, water flow, pump status.
4. Scenario EngineTest agronomic feasibility and rotation for each crop.
5. Margin & RiskCompute net income, water needs, and risk penalties.
6. Explainable resultShows gates, assumptions, margin, downside case, and ranking reason.
7. Farmer reviewReview one crop change in the 3D Stress Lab; no pump or valve commands.
