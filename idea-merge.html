NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · merged concept

Make limited land and every farming decision count.

Smallholder farmers with limited land can struggle to earn a stable income when water, weather, input costs, and selling prices shift. FieldShift compares feasible crop plans using NASA context, local economics, farmer constraints, and proposed field measurements.

Problem we address

Farmers need a clearer way to compare what to plant when water, weather, available capital, selling prices, and expected harvest can change. A high-yield crop does not always leave the strongest risk-adjusted income.

Feasible crop choicesWater and capital limitsStress testingGround-truth evidence
Decision objective
Risk-adjusted net margin / ha

First screen for soil, calendar, water, and capital feasibility. Then rank eligible plans by margin after a transparent downside reserve.

What merges

Three ideas, one reviewable decision.

Stress scenarios expose weak points; economics show the trade-offs; plot measurements help check regional context.

🧪

FarmShift · test stress in 3D

Replay 12 crop weeks in a 3D plant model. Compare the plan with one crop change under lower rainfall, limited irrigation, heat, price, and harvest assumptions.

Bring water reserve, capital limits, and a qualitative profitability context into the review. Stress Lab shows no money values and runs no margin formula.

Decision flow

Measure, compare, review.

1. Farmer & plotCrop history, area, capital, water budget, and priorities.
2. Earth dataNASA observations give regional weather and vegetation context.
3. IoT ground truthProbe, microclimate, flow, pump status, and surface photos.
4. Feasible planScreen water and capital limits; compare crop options.
5. Stress LabReplay the season in 3D and test one crop change.
6. Farmer reviewReview risk, reserve, assumptions, and evidence before deciding.
Evidence boundary: NASA data is regional context. Soil probes measure root-zone moisture; camera images show the surface only. Synthetic prototype data is labeled and does not claim live sensing or validated crop advice.

App workstream

  • One next-season crop decision for one example field.
  • Four climate presets, editable rainfall/irrigation/heat, and a 12-week 3D plant replay.
  • Compare the plan baseline with one crop change; price and harvest remain separate assumptions.
  • Stress Lab uses qualitative context and nonfinancial indices; plan ranking keeps its separate economic model.
